# Educational Resources

Ten rigorous, interactive graduate-level resources authored by Freddrick Logan on educational research, policy, leadership, and instructional design. Each is a live, interactive tool — not just an article.

**Resource hub:** https://freddricklogan.github.io/resources/

| Resource | Focus | Live |
|:---------|:------|:-----|
| Qualitative Research | Paradigms, the five approaches, sampling, coding & analysis, trustworthiness | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/qualitative-research-methods/) |
| Quantitative Inquiry in Education | Measurement, sampling, inference, effect size, critical reading of results | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/quantitative-inquiry/) |
| Educational Policy Analysis & Program Evaluation | Decision analysis, logic models, program evaluation, resource allocation, equity | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/educational-policy-analysis/) |
| Educational Leadership & School Improvement | Organizational theory, leadership, improvement science, education law, systemic equity | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/educational-leadership/) |
| Higher Education Leadership & Governance | Institution types, governance, finance, student development, legal foundations | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/higher-education-leadership/) |
| Global Higher Education & Internationalization | Internationalization, mobility, comparative systems, study-abroad leadership | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/global-higher-education/) |
| Teaching, Teacher Education & Curriculum | Pedagogy, teacher development, mathematics & science education, curriculum design | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/teaching-and-teacher-education/) |
| Museums & Informal Learning | Free-choice learning, the Contextual Model, science-learning strands, exhibit design | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/museums-informal-learning/) |
| Online Course Design Studio | Instructional design, assessment, HyFlex/blended, facilitation & communication | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/online-course-design/) |
| Accessibility & UDL Toolkit | WCAG, Universal Design for Learning, accessible documents & video, contrast checker | [Open](https://freddricklogan.github.io/accessibility-udl/) |
