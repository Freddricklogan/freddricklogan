## Executive Summary

Educational technologist and technology leader working at the intersection of systems, data, and public service. I turn complex, real-world problems into working systems that institutions actually adopt.

I build across the full stack &mdash; cloud infrastructure, data and machine learning, and full-stack applications &mdash; and I lead the instructional design, research, and policy work that helps organizations modernize without losing sight of the people they serve.

I also consult on cloud architecture, cybersecurity, and educational-technology strategy, and I am the author of ten interactive, graduate-level educational resources. The portfolio below represents more than fifty shipped projects and resources.
