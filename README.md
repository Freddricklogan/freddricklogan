## Executive Summary

Educational technologist and technology leader working at the intersection of systems, data, and public service. I turn complex, real-world problems into working systems that institutions actually adopt.

I build across the full stack &mdash; cloud infrastructure, data and machine learning, and full-stack applications &mdash; and I lead the instructional design, research, and policy work that makes technology land in education and government.

I am also the author of ten interactive, graduate-level educational resources spanning research methods, policy, leadership, and instructional design. The portfolio below represents more than forty-five shipped projects.
